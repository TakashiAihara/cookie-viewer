# Privacy policy

senbei reads the cookies of the tab you open it on, only after you grant that site, and only to display them in its popup.

- It does not send any data anywhere. The extension makes no network requests.
- It does not store any data. Nothing is written to extension storage, and the list is discarded when the popup closes.
- The only place a cookie value goes besides the popup is your clipboard, when you press "copy".
